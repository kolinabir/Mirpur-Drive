# Performance pass, 2026-09-21: low-end PCs at full quality

Goal: smooth on integrated graphics / old laptops without lowering what the
player sees up close. Follows docs/PERF-PASS-2026-09-20.md (MSAA, governor).

## Measured (Apple M4, 1600x900 CSS px, 1.5x, Pallabi spawn, same view)

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| draw calls | 403 | 239 |
| triangles submitted | 1,105k | 836k |
| three.js submission (CPU) | 1.31 ms | 0.66 ms |

The M4 is fill-rate bound and too fast for these to move its frame time; they
are the costs that scale badly on a slow CPU + integrated GPU. Not yet measured
on such a machine.

## What was wasted, and what changed

Nothing here is visible: every cut is of geometry that was under a pixel.

1. **Trains** were 6 trains x 6 cars x ~14 meshes = ~500 meshes, drawn in full
   wherever they were on the 4 km line. Past 350 m a car is now ONE merged,
   vertex-coloured mesh with the body's own steel material, plus the emissive
   parts (window band, lights, destination board) so night trains still glow.
   `buildTrain()` / `updateDetail()` in src/metro.js.
2. **Station fittings** (canopy ribs 15k tris each, PSD frames, roundels,
   downlights, trusses) are hidden beyond 700 m.
3. **Street lights and power poles**: 6,322 instances, 212k triangles, in
   map-wide InstancedMeshes that can never be frustum-culled. Now re-packed to
   the instances within 500 m whenever the camera moves 48 m
   (src/instance-cull.js). Same 5 draw calls. Destructible street lights edit
   instances through the culler, which keeps stable indices.
4. **Traffic** (previous commit): 43 -> 18 draw calls, far LOD beyond 70 m.

## Governor stage two

When a machine is still over budget at the resolution floor, the governor now
scales the DISTANCES above (and the building tile range, 750 m) down in 12.5%
steps to a floor of 50%, and restores them first when there is headroom. A
machine that holds 60 fps never enters it. `?debug` shows `detail NN%` when
active. Simulated: fast and medium GPUs never touch it; a slow one settles at
0.70x / 50%.

## Not done

- Pedestrians are 169k triangles (910 x 186) in one mesh with shader-animated
  limbs; a far LOD needs the same re-pack as traffic plus a second shader path.
- 4x MSAA is still on for low-DPI screens, which is where low-end PCs are. It
  is the largest single fill cost there, but turning it off is visible.
- Load time and texture memory: only the facade atlas is KTX2 (the other 39
  textures are JPGs that expand to full size in VRAM); the viaduct and streets
  are rebuilt on every visitor's CPU instead of baked; scene-north.json is one
  4.3 MB file rather than tiles.
