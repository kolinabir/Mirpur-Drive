# Road reference sources

New photos collected for this pass (in addition to reusing
`reference/metro/photos/26_arc_of_metro_bridge_at_dhaka_jpg.jpg` and
`reference/metro/photos/01_mirpur_10_metro_station_jpg.jpg`, already in the repo).

| File | Source page | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `mirpur_road_wikimedia.jpg` | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mirpur_Road.jpg | Older/low-res photo, dusty pale road in flat light. Low reliability for colour (looks bleached from film/scan exposure) but useful for surface texture and lack of kerb. |
| `mirpur_road_2_wikimedia.jpg` | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mirpur_Road_2.jpg | Elevated view of a Mirpur Road-type arterial at dusk: mid-grey asphalt, white dashed centre line, yellow/black-striped concrete median barrier, buses/CNGs, dust deposits along the barrier base. Best reference for "typical arterial in shade/dusk light". |
| `dhaka_roads_01_wikimedia.jpg` | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dhaka_City_area_Roads_in_Bangladesh_01.jpg | A painted parking/lane grid intersection in full midday sun: yellow box-grid and white dash lane paint, still fairly visible (less worn than the arterial default), road reads warm and sun-bleached. Best reference for "sun-bleached" and for lane-paint colour/condition. |

Also referenced from the existing metro photo set:
- `26_arc_of_metro_bridge_at_dhaka_jpg.jpg` — road passing directly under a
  completed viaduct arch, similar composition to our corridor. Warm mid-grey
  asphalt, no continuous kerb (dirt/rubble shoulder), dust-coloured edges,
  visible surface patching near the shop-front side.
- `01_mirpur_10_metro_station_jpg.jpg` — mostly station/bus imagery, minimal
  usable road surface, kept for context only.

License note: all four are Wikimedia Commons uploads (public domain or
CC-BY/CC-BY-SA per their file pages); the metro-photos set is credited in
`reference/metro/photos/CREDITS.md`. Reference only, not shipped in the
build.
