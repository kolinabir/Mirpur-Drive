# Mirpur 10 foot over bridge — observations, 20 September 2026

Owner asked for landmarks that look like the real place, and for the imagery
to be gathered here rather than supplied by hand. Per `reference/README.md`,
the durable output is this written spec; no third-party imagery is stored.

## Plan (surveyed)

Four arms radiating from ONE hub at scene (184.8, 735.4), over Mirpur 10
circle. Arm lengths 20.6 / 23.6 / 23.4 / 22.1 m. **It is an X, not a ring** —
worth stating because the obvious guess is a ring over a roundabout.

## Elevation (observed, April 2020)

- **Structure: red-oxide painted steel throughout.** Not galvanised, not
  green — the corridor's green corrugated roofing (see `../metro/`) does not
  appear on this bridge.
- **Railings:** a top rail, a mid rail and a low kerb rail between posts at
  roughly 2 m centres, with a **prominent X cross-brace** in the lower half
  of each bay. This is the bridge's most recognisable feature.
- **Deck:** buff/grey concrete with a **painted ochre centre strip** running
  the length of each arm.
- **Portal frames:** a **gabled red steel gate** with a shallow pitched roof
  and a small finial, standing over the walkway at the arm ends.
- **Stairs:** straight flights, steep, with the same red railings.
- **Occupation:** hawker stalls under blue and patterned tarpaulins stand
  along the deck edge, with goods laid out; a blue Dhaka North City
  Corporation waste drum at one landing. Deck litter is normal.

## Known gaps

- The photospheres are **April 2020**, when the viaduct was still under
  construction (segment-launching gantry visible). Anything about the
  bridge's *surroundings* from these views is out of date; only the bridge
  structure itself was used.
- Paint condition, current signage on the bridge, and whether the hawker
  stalls are still permitted are **unverified** for 2026.
- Not modelled yet: the tarpaulin stalls and the waste drums.
