## Part 3 — Mirpur 10 specifics

- Station sits essentially **on/immediately adjacent to the Mirpur 10 roundabout (Golchattar)**, directly beside the Fire Service (Bangladesh Fire Service & Civil Defence) station building at the circle. Model the station box running along the main north–south arterial (Mirpur Road / Rokeya Sarani axis) with its footprint overlapping the northern edge of the traffic circle.
- **4 entrances/exit legs**, distributed around the circle so pedestrians can reach each quadrant without crossing live traffic lanes at grade. One leg (marked "Exit B" on DMTCL wayfinding) points toward Sher-e-Bangla National Cricket Stadium (west, ~6 min walk).
- Surrounding landmarks to include as context blockout (not full detail): Fire Service HQ building (adjacent, distinctive red/white livery low-rise), Mirpur Plaza / Sony Square / Mukto Bangla shopping complexes (north/east quadrant, dense multi-storey commercial signage-heavy facades), Mirpur Indoor Stadium (south-west), dense informal market awnings at street level typical of "Hope Market"-type strip shops **[ESTIMATE placement — exact quadrant per landmark not confirmed by a single authoritative source; use for scene-dressing, not survey-accurate placement]**.
- Roads converging at the circle: at least 4–5 arms (Mirpur Road corridor running north toward Mirpur 11/12, south toward Kazipara/Shewrapara; Rokeya Sarani-type cross arms toward the stadium and toward Kafrul/Benarasi Palli direction east). Treat the circle as a large multi-arm roundabout with the station canopy spanning above the traffic island and the main road.

## Part 4 — Mirpur 11 specifics

- Station runs above **Mirpur Road**, straddling the corridor between the Mirpur 11 bus stand and the Section 11 commercial strip.
- **2 entrances only** — one on each side of the road (simpler footprint than Mirpur 10; no roundabout underneath, just a straight arterial with a central median).
- Landmarks: Mirpur 11 bus stand (~3 min walk, informal bus bay with roadside stalls) — good candidate for background clutter; Purobi Cinema Hall is a long-standing local landmark in this same corridor (older single-screen cinema building) — include as a nearby low-rise block if doing wider scene context, but its exact distance/side relative to the station entrances is **[ESTIMATE — not confirmed by DMTCL source]**.
- Station orientation: box girder and platform run parallel to Mirpur Road (roughly north–south, continuing the alignment from Mirpur 10 toward Pallabi).

## Part 6 — Per-element build checklist

1. **Pier shaft** — single RC column, Ø/width ≈ 2.0 m, height ≈ 13 m road-to-cap, painted off-white/light grey (`#DCDCD4`), plain cylindrical or lightly chamfered.
2. **Pier cap (hammerhead)** — flared cap ≈ 8 m wide × 1.8 m deep at top of shaft, same finish as shaft.
3. **Girder (box girder)** — precast segmental box, ≈ 9.7 m wide × 2.0 m deep, span ≈ 30 m between piers (shorten near stations), grey concrete (`#B8B8AE`), visible segment joints every ~2.5–3 m as a subtle panel line.
4. **Parapet** — ≈ 1.2 m high edge wall along both sides of the girder deck, same concrete tone, sometimes with a lighter cable-tray cover strip.
5. **OCS mast** — single steel cantilever mast per side roughly every span, dark grey/galvanized (`#6E7276`), portal/gantry frames only at station approaches and crossovers; contact wire height ≈ 5.4 m above rail.
6. **Track plinth** — twin rail, standard gauge 1.435 m, direct-fixation on low concrete plinths (~0.2 m), rail centres ≈ 3.9 m apart.
7. **Platform slab** — elevated ≈ 15 m above road, ≈ 180 m long × ~10–12 m wide per side platform **[ESTIMATE width]**.
8. **PSD (platform screen doors)** — full-height glass screen ≈ 2.0–2.1 m, aluminium frame (`#B0B4B8`), sliding doors aligned to train doors.
9. **Canopy** — curved standing-seam/corrugated metal roof on tubular steel truss columns, off-white (`#E6E6DE`), apex ≈ 6–7 m above platform, continuous skylight strip optional.
10. **Columns (platform/concourse structural)** — round or I-shaped steel/RC columns, painted white/light grey, spaced with the truss bays (~8–10 m).
11. **Concourse box** — glazed curtain-wall enclosure, ≈ 180 m long × ~20–24 m wide, light grey panel + blue-green tinted glass band (`#4E7A82`), dark grey mullions (`#3A3D40`).
12. **Glazing** — tinted blue-green laminated glass at concourse facade, clear/light glazing at platform windscreens (where present).
13. **Stairs** — open steel-frame stairs from footpath to concourse, non-slip tread, handrail in DMTCL green or stainless.
14. **Escalators** — paired (up/down) at each entrance leg and concourse-to-platform, stainless-steel balustrade.
15. **Lifts** — glazed lift shaft at each entrance leg + one on platform for accessibility, light grey/stainless cladding.
16. **Entrance pavilions** — ground-level canopy/roof over the stair/escalator/lift head at footpath level, lightweight steel-and-glass, DMTCL green accent trim, station name signage on the fascia.
17. **Name boards** — dark blue-green or green background (`#006747`), white Bangla text (large, top) + white English text (below/beside), DMTCL roundel/logo at one end; mounted on the platform canopy fascia and concourse facade.
18. **Train** — see Part 5 table; stainless body, green accent band, 4 doors/side/car, 6-car consist ≈ 120 m.

## ADDENDUM: corrections from the photo set (advisor review, overrides the estimates above)

These were read directly from `photos/01`, `13`, `24`, `26`, `32`. Where they
conflict with Parts 1 to 6 or the colour table, the addendum wins.

A1. **Concourse box is NOT a glass curtain wall.** At Mirpur 10 (photo 01) the
    concourse and stair cores are clad in RED-BROWN BRICK panels (`#9A4A32` to
    `#B0603E`) with pale concrete horizontal bands every ~1.2 m (`#D8D3C6`) and
    an exposed pale concrete frame of columns and edge beams (`#CFCBC0`). The
    glazing is limited to a horizontal window band at concourse level. Model:
    concrete frame + brick infill panels + band courses; use the CC0 brick map
    tinted to that red-brown.

A2. **DMTCL colour is GREEN, not teal.** Every trim, fascia edge, roundel and
    underline is `#0C7A4E` to `#006747`. Remove all uses of `#00A19A` teal.

A3. **Station name board**: white rounded "pill" (`#F2F2EE`) with black/dark
    green Bangla text on top, a thin green underline, English below; the DMTCL
    logo (stylised train in a green roundel) sits between the two names on the
    street fascia. On the platform, the pill is mounted inside a green ring
    (roundel) on the column, photo 13. Street-level fascia board is roughly
    6 m x 1 m.

A4. **Platform canopy**: shallow segmental arch of curved tubular steel
    trusses (grey `#8E9296`), corrugated metal deck on top, underside reads as
    dark ribbed metal (`#4B4F4A`) with a translucent skylight strip along the
    ridge (photo 24 and 32). A bright GREEN fascia/edge trim (`#0C7A4E`) runs
    along the eaves and gable ends; the top surface is off-white. Columns are
    round steel tubes ~0.45 m dia, painted light grey, spaced ~9 m, standing on
    the outer edge of each side platform. Apex ~6.5 m above platform.

A5. **Entrances**: green corrugated BARREL-VAULT canopies (`#2F8F5B`) over the
    stairs and footbridges to the footpaths (photo 01, left). The stair core is
    the same brick-and-concrete finish as the concourse.

A6. **Platform screen doors are HALF-HEIGHT** (~1.5 m), stainless steel frames
    with glass, not full height (photos 24, 32).

A7. **Platform floor**: dark grey polished stone tiles (`#5A5C5A`) with a
    yellow tactile strip ~0.6 m from the PSD line.

A8. **Viaduct girder** (photo 26): single-cell box, sloping webs, depth ~2.0 m,
    top width ~9.7 m, bottom width ~5.5 m, pale concrete `#C9C7BE` with faint
    vertical segment joint lines every ~3 m and grey rain streaks. Streetlight
    poles are mounted ON the deck parapet, not only on the road.

A9. **Pier at the station**: the girder haunches downward in a curved
    "hammerhead" into a rectangular pier with chamfered corners and shallow
    vertical flutes on the shaft (photo 01, right). Colour same pale concrete.
    Typical mid-span piers are the same rectangular chamfered shaft (not
    circular). At wide road crossings a two-column portal pier is used
    (photo 26); optional.

A10. **Train livery** (photo 32): stainless silver body (`#C8CACC`), a GREEN
    band (`#0C7A4E`) along the window belt full length, GREEN cab face with a
    WHITE lower nose panel, RED skirt/coupler cover and RED door edge stripes
    (`#C8302A`), black windscreen band, orange LED destination display. Not
    white-and-teal.

A11. **OCS**: rigid overhead conductor rail on portal gantries at stations and
    single cantilever masts on the viaduct, galvanised grey. Visible in every
    platform photo; must be modelled.
