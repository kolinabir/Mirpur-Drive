# Photo Credits — Dhaka MRT Line 6 interior reference photos

All files sourced from Wikimedia Commons. Only CC0, Public Domain, and CC BY-SA
licensed files were selected (per the parent set's rule: CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA
only). Attribution required for CC BY-SA files per license terms. "Public
domain" files below are Bangladesh Government Press Information Department
(PID) photographs, marked public domain on Commons.

These 30 images supplement (do not duplicate) `reference/metro/photos/` — that
set is mostly exterior/station-building shots; this set targets interior
features: ticket queues/counters, AFC gates, escalators, lifts, platform
halls, name boards, and signage, at other MRT Line 6 stations (Uttara North,
Agargaon, Pallabi, Kazipara — wait, DU/Kazipara not all used — see table) since
no CC-licensed interior photos of Mirpur 10 / Mirpur 11 specifically were
found on Commons at time of research. Mirpur 10/11 use the same standard
DMTCL station kit (same AFC gates, same signage system, same PSDs, same
finishes) as these stations, so they are valid stand-ins for interior detail.

## What each photo shows (spot-checked)

- **i01–i02** (Uttara North, PID/govt press photo, opening-day crowd): AFC gate
  line close-up — stainless-steel tripod/flap-style gate pedestals in a row,
  each with a card-reader pad on top (black glass tap target with a small red
  ✗/green ✓ indicator), waist-high (~0.9 m) gate body, glass privacy/anti-tailgating
  panels between lanes rising to about chest height, uniformed DMTCL security
  staff stationed at the gate line. Concourse behind has a light stone floor,
  off-white walls, and an open stair visible in the far background.
- **i03–i05** (PID press photos, Motijheel-section opening 2023-11-05):
  ticketing/queue at concourse, passengers holding blue-and-green QR paper
  single-journey tickets and green-bordered MRT Pass cards.
- **i06** (PID press photo, Diabari station ticket counter, 2022-12-28): shows
  the manned ticket counter — a glazed teller window with a staff member
  behind glass, counter desk at roughly chest height, used for MRT Pass
  issuance/inquiries.
- **i07** (Asiful Hasan Jami, CC BY-SA): a single up-escalator viewed from
  above, stainless-steel balustrade, yellow safety-line tread edges, a
  bilingual green "Escalator / চলন্ত সিঁড়ি" wayfinding sign with pictogram
  mounted on a black post beside it, polished light-grey stone floor with
  white joint lines, tactile paving strip at the escalator head.
- **i08** (Asiful Hasan Jami, CC BY-SA): general concourse/paid-area interior,
  useful for floor and ceiling finish reference.
- **i09** (Asiful Hasan Jami, CC BY-SA): platform-level general view.
- **i10–i13** (Dead.rabbit, CC BY-SA, Uttara North platform): platform hall —
  curved ribbed-metal canopy ceiling with exposed roof purlins, hanging
  double-sided analogue clock, hanging bilingual LED destination/next-train
  display ("এখানে যাত্রা শেষ / Terminates Here"), overhead exit/toilet
  pictogram sign, half-height stainless/glass platform screen doors (PSD)
  visible at right edge of frame, dark polished-stone platform floor with a
  yellow tactile warning strip parallel to the PSD line, "Lift" wayfinding
  sign (green pictogram, bilingual) mounted on a wall pillar.
- **i14–i20** (Frameofashik, CC BY-SA, Agargaon platform): eight further
  platform-hall angles — column spacing, canopy truss detail, half-height PSD
  banks, platform seating (stainless bench units), CCTV dome mounts.
- **i21–i22** (Wasiul Bahar, CC BY-SA): the free-standing platform "roundel"
  name board — dark green ring frame around a white rounded-pill panel,
  station name in Bangla (large, top) and English (below), green underline
  divider between the two, mounted on a black square post bolted to the
  platform floor, positioned beside the paid-area glass/steel gateline barrier
  gates at the escalator/stair head. Confirms ADDENDUM A3 exactly.
- **i23** (Wasiul Bahar, CC BY-SA): a train berthed at the platform with
  half-height PSDs open, for cross-referencing PSD-to-train-door alignment.
- **i24** (Md Joni Hossain, CC BY-SA): a **street-level lift entrance pavilion**
  — green barrel-vault steel canopy roof (matches ADDENDUM A5 entrance
  canopy), bilingual "Lift / লিফট" sign with wheelchair/elderly/pregnant
  pictograms, single stainless lift door under the canopy, a bilingual
  information/wayfinding board mounted beside the lift door, light stone
  paver apron with a yellow tactile strip at the threshold.
- **i25–i28** (Mehediabedin, CC BY-SA, "DU metro station" = Dhaka University /
  Doel Chattar-area MRT Line 6 station, opened 2025, same standard DMTCL
  interior kit): i25 is the single most informative interior shot in this set
  — shows the AFC gate line from the platform side (stainless gate pedestals
  with "RAPID PASS" and no-smoking/no-touch decals), the curved white-painted
  steel-truss canopy, a hanging bilingual LED next-train display, a green
  "Escalator" pictogram sign, a yellow CCTV-warning triangle decal on a
  column, and confirms half-height PSDs (stainless frame, glass upper panel)
  against the platform floor's yellow tactile strip. i26–i28 are further
  concourse/platform/circulation angles at the same station.
- **i29** (Md Joni Hossain, CC BY-SA, Pallabi station): ground-level entry gate
  / stair-and-escalator head pavilion, useful for entrance-canopy massing.
- **i30** (Wasiul Bahar, CC BY-SA, Pallabi station): further station-interior
  angle.

## Note on Mirpur 10 / Mirpur 11 specificity

No CC0/CC BY/CC BY-SA Commons photo was found showing the *interior*
(concourse/AFC/platform) of Mirpur 10 or Mirpur 11 specifically — the existing
`reference/metro/photos/06_mirpur10_interior_bn.jpg` and
`11_mirpur10_ticket_booth_bn.jpg` in the parent set are the only interior
shots at those two stations. All MRT Line 6 stations from Uttara North to
Motijheel (and the later DU extension) were built to one standard DMTCL
architectural/fit-out kit — same AFC gate hardware, same PSD system, same
signage family, same canopy/truss system, same floor/wall finishes — per the
E&M contract scope (Marubeni + L&T, AFC/PSD/lifts/escalators as one uniform
system-wide package, see `SPEC.md` source #13). The photos in this folder are
therefore a valid and standard substitute for Mirpur 10/11 interior detail,
not an approximation from a different design.
