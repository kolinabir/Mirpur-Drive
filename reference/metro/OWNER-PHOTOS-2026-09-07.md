# Owner-supplied photos, 2026-09-07 (12 images, seen in chat; not on disk)

The owner pasted 12 photos of Mirpur 10 and Line 6 interiors into the chat.
The files could not be saved from the chat, so this is the advisor's
transcription. Where it conflicts with SPEC.md / SPEC-INTERIOR.md, THIS
FILE WINS (it is Mirpur 10 itself, most other refs are other stations).

## Owner's explicit corrections
1. **Nothing stands under the metro except the road.** The viaduct runs
   down the centre of Begum Rokeya Sarani with traffic on both sides of
   every pier. Buildings that OSM places inside the corridor are wrong for
   the game and must be culled (measured: 23 footprints within 10 m of the
   centreline, 86 within 14 m).
2. The station has **two main floors**: a concourse (tickets, gates) and
   above it the platform level where trains stop. Street -> concourse ->
   platform.

## Second batch (9 images, ~16:15): platforms and viaduct

### Platform canopy, corrected from these photos (Q1-Q5)
- Structure: curved tubular steel SPACE-FRAME arches, light grey /
  galvanised (#B8BCBE), each arch a lattice truss (two chords with
  diagonal web tubes), every ~9 m; longitudinal purlins between arches;
  columns are round steel tubes at the platform's outer edge.
- Deck: corrugated metal seen from below; tone is MID GREY (#8A8D8A) to
  charcoal in shadow, not off-white and not black. Reads dark only where
  no light reaches. Advisor note: the "bright canopy" instruction in
  P0-E2b was too strong; the target is a mid-grey ribbed underside with
  grey lattice arches, lit by daylight from the open sides and skylights.
- SKYLIGHTS: long translucent glass strips along the ridge, framed in
  grey tubes, several panels long, sky visible through them (#A9D6DB
  tint). They are the main light source down the centre of the platform.
- Fixtures hanging from the trusses: rows of small round downlights along
  each side; LED passenger displays (black boxes, orange text: destination
  in Bangla + English, time); a round station clock; green direction
  boards ("Escalator", "Lift", "Emergency Exit") with white text and
  pictograms; speakers and CCTV.
### Platform surfaces and edge (Q1-Q5)
- Floor: polished dark grey granite tiles (#4F5250), quite reflective.
  Yellow tactile strip ~0.6 m back from the PSD line, plus yellow
  door-position markings; orange painted arrows on the floor.
- PSDs: half-height (~1.5 m) brushed stainless posts and frames with
  glass panels between; sliding gate leaves aligned to train doors; AD
  PANELS mounted on the PSD faces; small labels "Car 6 - Door 1" and
  prohibition stickers. On the platform side, a continuous stainless
  handrail.
- Outer platform edge: low wall + glazed/louvred windows or a glass
  balustrade with stainless rail; daylight enters low from the sides.
- Furniture: stainless benches; grey vented equipment cabinets; a lift
  core with stainless/grey doors, cream tiled walls; green roundel sign
  on a post; fire extinguisher boxes; yellow CCTV warning triangles.

### Viaduct and OCS (Q6-Q9)
- Deck: twin ballastless slab tracks on a pale concrete box girder,
  solid parapets ~1.2 m in pale concrete with a cable trough/walkway
  along each edge; light grey noise-barrier posts on some stretches.
- OCS: galvanised light-grey MASTS every ~30 m on BOTH parapets, each
  with a cantilever arm over its track; rigid conductor bar; at intervals
  the two masts are joined by a cross-beam (portal). Masts are the most
  visible feature of the viaduct from any angle: model them all.
- Train from above: silver roof with AC units per car, six cars; green
  band along the windows; green cab with white V nose panel and red
  skirt. Trains run on the LEFT track relative to direction? (unknown;
  Line 6 runs left-hand; do not assert.)
- Street below the viaduct (Q9): dense 5-8 storey buildings right up to
  the footpath, rooftops with water tanks; single-lane traffic each side
  around the piers; hawkers on the footpaths.
