# Privacy — Mirpur Drive

> Mirpur Drive — Walk, drive and ride the metro through a 3D Mirpur, Dhaka — free, in your browser.
> Canonical: https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/privacy/

Mirpur Drive has no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies. What is stored in your browser, what analytics are collected and which third parties are contacted.

## The short version

Mirpur Drive has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking cookies. It does not ask for your name, email, location or any other personal information, and nothing you do in the game is sent to a server. The game is a set of static files; there is no backend that could store anything about you.

## What is stored on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember a few things between visits: your on-foot progress in each district (wallet, food diary and places discovered), whether you prefer the third-person camera, and an optional developer debug flag. This data never leaves your browser. Clearing site data for this domain in your browser settings removes all of it.

## Analytics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and measure loading performance. It does not use cookies or local storage, does not fingerprint visitors and does not track people across sites. The author sees totals such as page views, referring sites, countries, browsers and page-load timings, never individual visitors.

## Third parties your browser contacts

The site is served by Cloudflare, which, like any web host, processes your IP address to deliver the files and protect the service. One web font (Noto Sans Bengali) is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google when the page loads. Links to GitHub and OpenStreetMap open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

## Children

The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, no purchases and no violence beyond knocking over a streetlight with a car. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
