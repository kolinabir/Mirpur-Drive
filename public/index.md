# Mirpur Drive

> Walk, drive and ride the metro through a 3D Mirpur, Dhaka — free, in your browser.

Mirpur Drive is a free, open-source 3D reconstruction of the Mirpur corridor along MRT Line 6 in Dhaka, Bangladesh. It runs in any modern desktop or mobile browser with WebGL; there is nothing to install and no account.

- Play: https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/
- Source code (MIT): https://github.com/kolinabir/Mirpur-Drive
- Author: Abir Kolin (https://github.com/kolinabir)

## What you can do

- Walk the real street grid (W A S D, mouse to look), or press V to drive a car through traffic.
- Ride MRT Line 6: walk into a station, pass the gates, board at the platform screen doors (E).
- Hail a rickshaw, CNG or bus, stop at cha stalls and keep a journal (J).
- Teleport to any station or landmark (O), open the full map (M), cycle the time of day (T).

## Places

- [Mirpur 10 Metro Station](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/mirpur-10-metro-station/): The elevated MRT Line 6 station above Mirpur 10 circle, modelled with walkable entrances, concourse, ticket gates and platforms.
- [Mirpur 11 Metro Station](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/mirpur-11-metro-station/): Mirpur 11 station and the long straight of Begum Rokeya Avenue beneath the viaduct — the best stretch in the game for driving.
- [Pallabi Metro Station](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/pallabi-metro-station/): Where every new game starts: Pallabi station and the Mirpur 12 shopping frontage, rebuilt sign by sign.
- [Uttara South Metro Station](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/uttara-south-metro-station/): The northern end of the playable map, where the city thins out and the viaduct crosses open land.
- [Agargaon Metro Station](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/agargaon-metro-station/): The first station of the second district, among the wide roads and government offices of Agargaon.
- [Bijoy Sarani Metro Station](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/bijoy-sarani-metro-station/): The station for Parliament: Bijoy Sarani, beside the open ground north of Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban.
- [Farmgate Metro Station](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/farmgate-metro-station/): The southern edge of the game world, where the metro corridor meets central Dhaka.
- [Mirpur 10 Golchokkor](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/mirpur-10-golchokkor/): The roundabout at the heart of Mirpur, under the station, where four roads and a footbridge meet.
- [Mirpur 10 Foot Over Bridge](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/mirpur-10-foot-over-bridge/): The four-armed red steel footbridge over Mirpur 10 circle, walkable end to end beneath the viaduct.
- [Sher-e-Bangla National Cricket Stadium](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/sher-e-bangla-cricket-stadium/): The home of Bangladesh cricket, modelled as a full bowl with stands, floodlight towers and a pitch you can walk onto.
- [Mirpur Benarasi Palli](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/benarasi-palli/): The Benarasi saree market of Mirpur Section 10: the maroon gate across the road and the banded colonnade of saree shops.
- [Pallabi & Mirpur 12 Street Frontage](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/pallabi-mirpur-12-street/): A real street corner rebuilt sign for sign: the food tower, the school and the shopping plaza on the Pallabi stretch.
- [Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban (National Parliament)](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/jatiya-sangsad-bhaban/): Louis Kahn's National Parliament House, rising from its lake at Sher-e-Bangla Nagar.
- [Manik Mia Avenue](https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/places/manik-mia-avenue/): The broad ceremonial avenue along the south side of the Parliament grounds.

## How it is made

Streets, building footprints and the metro alignment come from OpenStreetMap (ODbL). Building heights come mostly from Google Open Buildings 2.5D (CC-BY-4.0). Stations and landmarks are modelled by hand from photographs. Built with Three.js and Vite; hosted as static files on Cloudflare.
