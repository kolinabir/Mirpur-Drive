Places in the game

Every station and landmark you can visit in Mirpur Drive: seven MRT Line 6 stations, the cricket stadium, Parliament, Benarasi Palli and more, each with a link that opens the game there.

MRT Line 6 stations

  • Mirpur 10 Metro Station

    The elevated MRT Line 6 station above Mirpur 10 circle, modelled with walkable entrances, concourse, ticket gates and platforms.

  • Mirpur 11 Metro Station

    Mirpur 11 station and the long straight of Begum Rokeya Avenue beneath the viaduct — the best stretch in the game for driving.

  • Pallabi Metro Station

    Where every new game starts: Pallabi station and the Mirpur 12 shopping frontage, rebuilt sign by sign.

  • Uttara South Metro Station

    The northern end of the playable map, where the city thins out and the viaduct crosses open land.

  • Agargaon Metro Station

    The first station of the second district, among the wide roads and government offices of Agargaon.

  • Bijoy Sarani Metro Station

    The station for Parliament: Bijoy Sarani, beside the open ground north of Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban.

  • Farmgate Metro Station

    The southern edge of the game world, where the metro corridor meets central Dhaka.

Landmarks and streets

  • Mirpur 10 Golchokkor

    The roundabout at the heart of Mirpur, under the station, where four roads and a footbridge meet.

  • Mirpur 10 Foot Over Bridge

    The four-armed red steel footbridge over Mirpur 10 circle, walkable end to end beneath the viaduct.

  • Sher-e-Bangla National Cricket Stadium

    The home of Bangladesh cricket, modelled as a full bowl with stands, floodlight towers and a pitch you can walk onto.

  • Mirpur Benarasi Palli

    The Benarasi saree market of Mirpur Section 10: the maroon gate across the road and the banded colonnade of saree shops.

  • Pallabi & Mirpur 12 Street Frontage

    A real street corner rebuilt sign for sign: the food tower, the school and the shopping plaza on the Pallabi stretch.

  • Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban (National Parliament)

    Louis Kahn's National Parliament House, rising from its lake at Sher-e-Bangla Nagar.

  • Manik Mia Avenue

    The broad ceremonial avenue along the south side of the Parliament grounds.

About the map

Streets, building footprints and the metro alignment come from OpenStreetMap; building heights mostly from Google's Open Buildings dataset. Stations and landmarks are modelled by hand from photographs. The Kazipara and Shewrapara stations between the two districts are not modelled yet. More about how it is made.
