# Contact — Mirpur Drive

> Mirpur Drive — Walk, drive and ride the metro through a 3D Mirpur, Dhaka — free, in your browser.
> Canonical: https://mirpurdrive.recalfy.com/contact/

How to report a bug, point out something that does not match the real Mirpur, suggest a landmark or reach the author of Mirpur Drive.

## The best way to reach the project

Mirpur Drive is developed in public, and the fastest way to get a reply is to open an issue on GitHub. Issues are read by the author and are visible to other contributors, so an answer helps everyone who has the same question.

## Reporting a bug

Please say where you were (press M and describe the spot, or name the nearest station), whether you were walking, driving or on the metro, the in-game time of day, and your device and browser. The line at the top of the screen shows the frame rate and a number ending in x, the render scale; include both if your report is about performance.

## "This does not look like the real place"

These reports are especially welcome. Name the place, say what the game shows and what is really there, and how you know: you live nearby, your own photo, an OpenStreetMap tag. Please link to photos rather than attaching images you did not take. If the problem is in the map data itself, fixing it on OpenStreetMap helps every project that uses it, including this one.

## Security and privacy concerns

If you have found a security problem, or you are a business or person shown in the game and want something changed or removed, open an issue, or contact the author through the GitHub profile linked below if it should not be public. Requests to remove a name are honoured.
